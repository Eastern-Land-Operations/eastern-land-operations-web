07 — Rules

Locked behaviors.
No exceptions.

Do
  • Use DM Mono for every Circuit ID, coordinate, figure, and reference number
  • Track all-caps Inter labels at 0.16em minimum
  • Track display Space Grotesk negatively at all sizes above 20px
  • Use Inter 400 for all running body text — no exceptions
  • Maintain 1.65+ line-height on all body copy
  • Keep all-caps labels under 20 characters — break into two lines if longer
  • Use Space Grotesk 700 for all primary headlines and cover copy
Do Not
  • Italicize Space Grotesk in any brand material
  • Use Space Grotesk for body copy or labels
  • Use DM Mono for headlines, body text, or labels
  • Mix positive and negative tracking within the same element
  • Use Inter in all-caps for anything longer than a label
  • Track body copy out — Inter at 0 tracking is the rule
  • Use weights above 600 in Inter — that role belongs to Space Grotesk
  • Introduce any fourth typeface for any reason
