03 — Layout Language

Editorial. Infrastructural.
Luxury through restraint.

Large Margins
Generous whitespace is the primary signal of control and confidence. Never crowd the page.
Strong Alignment
Every element anchors to a clear grid. Inconsistent alignment reads as amateur regardless of color or type choices.
Minimal Decoration
No ornamental elements. Thin dividers and rules only when they serve structure, not style.
Monochrome Dominance
Black, graphite, and off-white carry the majority of every layout. Olive appears sparingly — never as a fill, only as an accent.
Intentional Whitespace
Space between elements should feel considered. When in doubt, add more breathing room rather than less.
Thin Grid Systems
1px rules in gunmetal. No shadow, no glow, no border-radius beyond 2px. The grid is infrastructure, not decoration.
